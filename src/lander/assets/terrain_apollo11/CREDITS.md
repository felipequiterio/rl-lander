# Apollo 11 landing site (Tranquility Base), Mare Tranquillitatis

Derived from NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) Narrow Angle Camera products
NAC_DTM_APOLLO11, NAC_DTM_APOLLO11_M150361817_50CM, from the Planetary Data System.

Credit: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University. NASA imagery is in the public domain.

Changes: cropped around the landing site, resampled, contrast-normalised, saved as JPEG (imagery)
and 16-bit PNG (terrain heights).
