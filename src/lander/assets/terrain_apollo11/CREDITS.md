# Apollo 11 landing site imagery

Derived from NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) Narrow Angle Camera products
NAC_DTM_APOLLO11 (terrain model, 2 m/px) and NAC_DTM_APOLLO11_M150361817_50CM (orthophoto,
0.5 m/px), from the Planetary Data System.

Credit: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University. NASA imagery is in the public domain.

Changes: cropped around the Apollo 11 landing site, resampled, contrast-normalised, saved as JPEG.
